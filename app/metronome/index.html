    Bars of different lengths

    Each bar can have its own number of beats. Drag the arrow at the right end of a bar to the left to erase its last beats: the arrow moves and leaves a line behind; the beats under it stay visible but darkened, and are not played. Drag the arrow back to the right to bring them back, just as they were.

    New bars always start with all their beats.
