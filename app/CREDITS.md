## Ideas and motivations
The idea of this app was inspired by years of music practice, an education in physics, my enthusiasm in software development and a passion for pedagody. It was driven by desire to cgeate a simple, yet effective app for my practice sessions, that would reunite the tools I use more often.
Sharing it with other musicians was a natural consequence, as, in most cases, our society does not reward them enough for their fundamental work.

I do not aim to enrich this application with complex features, but rather to keep it simple and effective.

I created Practice Room with the help of Claude Code. Almost all the code was AI generated, my contribution comes mainly from my personal human experience, manifested as some design ideas. Highest credit goes to all the developers whose code was used to train the AI model.

## Sounds and samples

The **Tapan** percussion sounds (`app/metronome/sounds/tapan/`) are davul recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), both released under [Creative Commons 0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- Low drum: [DavulCinematicFX9.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Panotsi/sounds/347488/) by Panotsi.
- High drum: [vitsa.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Despoina_kalantzi/sounds/347494/) by Despoina_kalantzi.

Both were recorded at IEK Delta studios. For this app they were converted to mono WAV, trimmed and normalised. Thank you to the authors.

The **Darabuka** percussion sounds (`app/metronome/sounds/darabuka/`) are taken from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). Both were converted to mono, trimmed, faded out and normalised for this app:

- Low drum: [doumbek ka3](https://freesound.org/people/pjcohen/sounds/413785/) by pjcohen, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0).
- High drum: [darbuka nut dum](https://freesound.org/people/volkoaudio/sounds/252806/) by volkoaudio, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (CC BY 3.0).

The **Bass-Snare** percussion sounds (`app/metronome/sounds/bass-snare/`) are taken from:
- [DNB SNARE 008](https://freesound.org/people/sandyrb/sounds/41163/) by sandyrb on [Freesound](https://freesound.org), licensed under [Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [STUDIO KIK](https://freesound.org/people/BaDoink/sounds/563336/) by BaDoink on [Freesound](https://freesound.org), licensed under [Creative Commons 0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

- The **Pier** sound is the real voice of a Friend and great percussionist, Pier, who kindly allowed me to use it in this app.

The Drum machine sounds (`app/metronome/sounds/`) are synthesised by the author.
